## Brand & Style

This design system targets modern B2B Go-To-Market, RevOps, and marketing executive teams who require absolute certainty from their attribution and pipeline data. The visual style embodies technical precision, clarity, and computational authority.

By marrying high-density data legibility with crisp visual restraint, the interface instills confidence and speed. The aesthetic leverages a hyper-clean corporate tech direction: pure neutral white canvas foundations, structural subtle gray borders, deep midnight typography, and high-energy electric blue accents that guide executive decision-making. Surface elements remain lean, planar, and light, utilizing minimal elevation and disciplined micro-radii to reinforce developer-grade craftsmanship and rigorous analytical power.

## Colors

The palette establishes an ultra-clean, high-contrast operational environment optimized for complex multi-touch attribution and metric readability.

- **Primary (`#2563EB` & `#335CFF`):** High-octane cobalt blues dedicated to key actions, primary data paths, interactive attribution nodes, and highlighted journey states.
- **Secondary (`#042652`):** Dense navy anchor hue utilized for technical feature backdrops, dark-mode preview panels, and high-impact structural containers.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#FFFFFF` to `#F8FAFC`):** Pure white canvas paired with tinted slate-gray backgrounds to establish clear visual segmentation without resorting to heavy strokes or aggressive shadows.
- **Text & Borders (`#0F172A`, `#475569`, `#E2E8F0`):** Razor-sharp, near-black slate typography ensures legibility across dense analytic tables and charts. Structural borders utilize low-opacity slate lines (`#E2E8F0`) to construct modular cards and metric dividers.

## Typography

The typography pairs `Geist` for headlines, numeric callouts, and interface controls with `Hanken Grotesk` for continuous body text and analytical descriptions.

- **Geist (Headlines & Labels):** Provides an architectural, developer-centric cadence. Tight negative tracking across `display`, `headline-lg`, and `headline-md` yields an assertive, premium SaaS aesthetic.
- **Hanken Grotesk (Body):** Delivers open counters and neutral proportions that sustain comfort and readability through long-form reports, cohort definitions, and complex customer journey narratives.
- **Data Callouts & Monospace Touchpoints:** All metric numbers, percentages, and delta indicators use tabular figures with Geist to guarantee aligned vertical scanning across data tables and KPI cards.

## Layout & Spacing

The spatial architecture balances high-density analytical dashboards with breathing room on marketing and reporting canvases.

- **Grid Discipline:** The standard layout follows a 12-column responsive fluid grid with 1.5rem (24px) gutters on desktop, collapsing to 8 columns on tablet, and 4 columns on mobile with 1rem (16px) gutters.
- **Rhythm & Alignment:** Spacing conforms to a rigorous 4px / 8px baseline. Micro-spacing (`space-xs` to `space-sm`) handles tight component internal padding such as pill badges, status tags, and button groups.
- **Card Breathing Room:** Dashboards and informational cards employ generous padding (`space-lg` to `space-xl`) paired with hairline divider boundaries to maintain separation between metrics without causing cognitive fatigue.

## Elevation & Depth

This system intentionally departs from heavy skeuomorphic shadows in favor of precise planar surfaces, low-contrast structural outlines, and subtle ambient light diffusion.

- **Level 0 (Flat Canvas):** Default base surfaces (`#FFFFFF` or `#F8FAFC`) with zero shadow. Separation is achieved strictly through `1px solid #E2E8F0` borders.
- **Level 1 (Card & Modular Panel):** Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, ambient shadow `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Hover & Interactive Cards):** Shadow transitions slightly outward on hover: `0 4px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`. Border shifts smoothly to `border-slate-300`.
- **Level 3 (Dropdowns & Popovers):** Elevated overlays receive `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)` with a hairline border `#E2E8F0`.
- **Brand Glows:** Critical visual highlights and Sankey chart connection endpoints leverage electric blue ambient glows: `0 0 24px -4px rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.25)`.

## Shapes

The design system maintains a refined, technical posture with controlled, soft roundedness (`level 1`).

- **Core Geometry:** Standard components—including cards, input fields, containers, and primary buttons—utilize `0.25rem` (4px) to `0.375rem` (6px) border radii. Larger dashboard containers step up to `rounded-lg` (`0.5rem` / 8px).
- **Pill Badges:** As an explicit functional exception, status pills, stage indicators, and category tag tokens utilize fully rounded capsule shapes (`9999px`) to create an immediate visual contrast against structural rectangular cards.
- **Border Treatment:** All surface divisions use crisp 1px borders, avoiding thick contours to safeguard data density and modern minimalism.

## Components

### Buttons

- **Primary:** Solid electric blue (`#2563EB`) background, white text (`#FFFFFF`), `font-weight: 500`, `font-size: 0.875rem`, subtle radius (`0.25rem` to `0.375rem`), with vertical padding `0.5rem` and horizontal `1rem`. Active hover transition to `#1D4ED8` with zero vertical displacement.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Canvas background (`#FFFFFF`), crisp border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, slate text (`#0F172A`). Hover state subtly shifts background to `#F8FAFC` and border to `#CBD5E1`.
- **Icon Buttons:** Fixed square aspect (32x32px or 36x36px), centered SVG glyphs, border radius matching system default (`0.25rem`).

### Chips & Badges

- **Status Pills:** Fully rounded (`rounded-full`), micro typography (`label-sm`), uppercase with slight letter spacing. Low-saturation background tints (e.g., `#EFF6FF` for blue brand chips, `#ECFDF5` for active cohort pills) paired with high-contrast text (`#1D4ED8` or `#065F46`).
- **Feature Category Tags:** Subtle light-gray fill (`#F1F5F9`), slate text (`#475569`), border `1px solid #E2E8F0`.

### Cards & Data Panels

- **Structure:** Crisp white background (`#FFFFFF`), `1px solid #E2E8F0`, padding `1.5rem`. Header areas clearly bifurcated with metric title on the left and contextual filters or time-range pill on the right.
- **Attribution & Flow Nodes:** Internal modular blocks inside cards use light contrast canvas (`#F8FAFC`) with razor-thin borders to demarcate stage touchpoints along customer journeys.

### Form Inputs

- **Text Inputs:** Height 40px, font `Hanken Grotesk`, size `0.875rem`. Border `1px solid #CBD5E1` on pure white background. Focused state displays a crisp 1px electric blue ring (`#2563EB`) and matching border color without fuzzy drop shadows.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Minimal `16x16px` bounds with `0.125rem` radius for checkboxes and circular for radios. Inactive state features `1.5px solid #CBD5E1`; checked state fills `#2563EB` with pure white check iconography.

### Data Tables & Journey Streams

- **Table Headers:** Background `#F8FAFC`, uppercase label typography (`label-sm`), border bottom `1px solid #E2E8F0`.
- **Table Rows:** Alternating hover highlight (`#F8FAFC/60`), height 44px, bottom border hairline `#F1F5F9`. Numbers aligned right with tabular lining figures.
